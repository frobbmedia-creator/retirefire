# RetireFire 90-day SEO operating plan

This is the repeatable distribution and measurement layer for the pages,
calculators, research downloads, and articles already published on
RetireFire.net.

## Success measures

Review these in Google Search Console every Monday:

1. Non-branded clicks and impressions
2. Number of queries in positions 4–20
3. Indexed pages versus the 63 canonical sitemap URLs
4. Click-through rate by page and query
5. Pages with impressions but zero clicks
6. Calculator-to-guide and guide-to-calculator clicks in Vercel Analytics

Use 28-day versus previous-28-day comparisons. Do not judge a new page from a
single day or week. Record the baseline on day 1, then checkpoints on days 30,
60, and 90.

## Weekly cadence

### Monday: measure and choose

- Export Search Console pages and queries for the last 28 days.
- Select one page ranking in positions 4–20 with meaningful impressions.
- Select one page with impressions but weak click-through rate.
- Confirm sitemap coverage and investigate any new excluded URL.

### Tuesday: improve one existing page

- Match the title and opening paragraph to the actual queries receiving
  impressions.
- Add one decision table, worked example, or calculator link.
- Add two contextual internal links from relevant articles or guides.
- Update `dateModified` only when the page materially changes.

### Wednesday: distribute one useful asset

Share a calculator result, table, or chart—not a generic homepage link. Rotate:

- Retirement-age estimate
- $1 million or $2 million readiness scenario
- Coast FIRE by age table
- One-year-of-waiting comparison
- Pre-Medicare healthcare bridge
- Withdrawal guardrail example

Use the canonical page URL and answer the community’s question in the post
itself. Disclose the RetireFire connection. Never mass-post or manufacture
engagement.

### Thursday: earn one relevant citation

Contact one retirement newsletter, independent financial educator, benefits
writer, or FIRE community curator. Offer a directly relevant calculator or CSV
as a source. Personalize the note around their existing work.

### Friday: maintenance

- Check the live sitemap and top landing pages.
- Review broken links and unexpected 404s.
- Submit materially updated URLs through IndexNow.
- Record changes, distribution activity, and outcomes.

[canonical URL]

[research or methodology URL]

If it strengthens a future update, feel free to cite or embed the result. I’m
connected with RetireFire, and there is no affiliate arrangement.

## Community post structure

1. Lead with a real decision: “What changes if retirement moves from 50 to 55?”
2. Give the answer or worked scenario in the post.
3. State the assumptions.
4. Link to the exact calculator or data table for readers who want to change
   the inputs.
5. Ask one substantive follow-up question.

## Page-improvement rules

- Prefer updating a relevant page over creating a near-duplicate.
- Do not publish programmatic permutations with only age or dollar values
  changed.
- Every indexable page must have a unique title, description, canonical URL,
  useful body copy, and a next-step link.
- Financial claims need a primary or authoritative source where applicable.
- Do not promise returns, guarantee retirement safety, or present simplified
  tax and healthcare estimates as advice.
- Preserve the site’s strongest differentiator: transparent assumptions and
  useful calculations without email capture.

## Day 30, 60, and 90 decisions

At each checkpoint, group pages into:

- **Expand:** positions 4–20 with growing impressions; add examples and links.
- **Rewrite:** impressions with weak CTR or mismatched queries; improve title,
  opening, and intent match.
- **Consolidate:** overlapping pages competing for the same query.
- **Hold:** indexed but too new or too low-volume to judge.

Create the next page only when Search Console reveals a distinct unanswered
decision. The default action is to improve a proven cluster, not inflate the
URL count.
